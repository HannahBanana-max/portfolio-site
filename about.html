About me

Where I’m from

I was born and raised in Le Havre. I still miss the sea sometimes, but Parisian architecture makes up for it. I remember my mum always taking photos, and when I was old enough, she let me take a few myself. I think that’s when my love for photography started. It was the same with museums. As a kid, I hated being dragged through them. Now, I rarely spend a weekend without visiting an exhibition. Thanks, Mum and Dad, for teaching me to open my eyes, stay curious, and understand how others see the world.

What I do now

I’m a Product Designer at @Fintecture, where I design tools that help businesses in their daily work. I work closely with developers, PMs, and stakeholders to build meaningful features, improve user experience, and contribute to our design system. Before that, I worked as a graphic designer, mostly in print and textile, where I learned creativity, storytelling, brand thinking, and adaptability. Today, I keep exploring creativity not just in my job, but also in my personal life through lots of crafty and DIY projects.

What I am looking for

Designing with purpose, having real impact, solving complex and meaningful problems, collaborating with talented, diverse teammates, learning from others, feeling aligned with a company’s values. It might sound cheesy or idealistic, but that’s me being honest. What I’m looking for is simply what makes me happy at work.
